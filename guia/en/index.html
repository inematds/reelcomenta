In short

reelcomenta is a command-line tool that takes the link of someone else's reel, TikTok or Short and builds a vertical video of your own commenting on that topic. The original video keeps playing, with its music turned down. Your HeyGen avatar speaks in a corner of the screen, the numbers from your research appear in a panel with their source, and the video ends with a question and a call to the free courses at inema.club. It is for people who produce content and want to ride a video that is already circulating, without recording anything. It needs Python, ffmpeg, a HeyGen account with your avatar, and the cardshorts and explicavideos projects.

What it is

One format, always the same rhythm

It was born from the "robot on stage" video (10/09/2026): a robot dancing with a dancer became the hook to talk about jobs replaced by AI and robots.

🎬 The reel stays on air

The first pass is the whole video; then the best excerpt comes back in slow motion while the numbers come in. The original music stays low and drops even lower when you speak.

📊 Numbers with a source

Each number appears in a panel with a counter, on the exact word where the avatar speaks of it, and carries its source underneath (report and year).

🧑‍💼 You in the corner

The avatar comes in through the HeyGen studio, automatically cropped to the face, with a frame and a name, without covering the video.

How it works

From the link to the MP4 on Telegram

Everything lives in one folder per edition inside edicoes/. The only text you write is roteiro.yaml.

Reel link→ Download + transcription→ roteiro.yaml (speech + numbers)→ Preview→ Avatar in the HeyGen studio→ Assembly→ Telegram bot

Opening

Frame 0 already full: date dd/mm/yyyy, a big headline and the reel running.

Numbers

3 to 4 cards with an animated counter, a short label and the source.

Question

"Will you keep waiting or will you get qualified?" — "ESPERAR" (wait) is struck through at the turning point.

Closing

A mosaic of the real covers of the portal's courses scrolling by, and the final INEMA.CLUB card.

Prerequisites

What needs to be installed

It runs locally. The only outside piece is the HeyGen studio, used through your subscription in the browser.

Python and ffmpeg

Python 3 with Pillow, PyYAML and OpenCV (face crop), ffmpeg and yt-dlp.

pip install pillow pyyaml opencv-python yt-dlp
sudo apt install ffmpeg

cardshorts

Local transcription with per-word timing and delivery to Telegram come from cardshorts.

git clone https://github.com/inematds/cardshorts

Avatar

explicavideos brings the HeyGen studio robot (Playwright with your logged-in profile) and the status reader.

# paths via variables, if they are elsewhere
export RC_EXPLICAVIDEOS=~/projetos/explicavideos
export RC_PERFIL_HEYGEN=~/.cache/inemaccbot/perfil-heygen
User guide · step by step

One edition, from link to video

In Claude Code or Codex, the reelcomenta skill does all the steps when you send the link. By hand, it goes like this:

1

Download the reel

Creates the edition folder, downloads the video, makes a 12-frame sheet, transcribes the audio and leaves the script template.

git clone https://github.com/inematds/reelcomenta && cd reelcomenta
python3 reelcomenta.py novo "https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeQ62SgBblE/" --id robo-trabalho
2

Research the numbers and write the script

3 to 4 numbers with source and year. The speech is 550 to 750 characters, with years and numbers written out in full. Each card enters on a word of the speech (the anchor).

# edicoes/2026-10-09-robo-trabalho/roteiro.yaml (excerpt)
fala: |
  Olha isso. O robô já divide o palco com a dançarina. ...
cartoes:
  - {ancora: noventa, numero: "{} MILHÕES", conta: 92,
     rotulo: "de empregos eliminados\naté 2030", fonte: "Fórum Econômico Mundial · 2025"}
pergunta: {ancora: pergunto, virada: qualific, riscado: ESPERAR, depois: ["OU SE", "QUALIFICAR"]}
cta: {ancora: inema, linha1: "+{cursos_100} CURSOS", linha2: GRATUITOS}
3

Check the preview

Draws the screens with estimated timing over frames of the reel, before spending the avatar queue.

python3 reelcomenta.py previa edicoes/2026-10-09-robo-trabalho   # → previa.jpg
4

Generate the avatar

Only runs with the approval file. Sends the speech to the HeyGen studio, checks the status and downloads the MP4 (30 min to 1 h in the queue).

echo "aprovado por Nei em 09/10/2026" > edicoes/2026-10-09-robo-trabalho/avatar/APROVADO_HEYGEN
python3 reelcomenta.py avatar edicoes/2026-10-09-robo-trabalho
5

Assemble, check and send

Assembly stops if the speech heard differs from the script. The check makes a 10-frame sheet and measures the volume.

python3 reelcomenta.py montar edicoes/2026-10-09-robo-trabalho    # → video.mp4
python3 reelcomenta.py conferir edicoes/2026-10-09-robo-trabalho  # → conferencia.jpg
python3 reelcomenta.py enviar edicoes/2026-10-09-robo-trabalho "legenda"
